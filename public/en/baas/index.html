Before choosing BAAS.

Is the monthly fee a motorcycle instalment?

No. The fee shown is for the battery subscription. The motorcycle is purchased separately. Request a Bali BAAS quotation for the motorcycle price and total upfront cost.

Which models are included?

The BAAS material lists Victory Standard and Extended, Athena Standard, and EdPower Standard. Bees and an Athena Extended rate are not listed.

What are the subscription and warranty terms?

Before buying, ask the Bali team for the minimum term, payment and cancellation rules, warranty and servicing coverage, and charging incentive conditions. These details are not specified in the programme banner.
