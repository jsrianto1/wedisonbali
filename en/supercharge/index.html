YOUR FIRST TIME?

Start with
the right steps.

  1. 01

    Check your model and location.

    Check that your bike supports SuperCharge. View location details in the app or confirm with the Bali team.

  2. 02

    Follow the on-site instructions.

    Park safely, then follow the instructions on the charger and in the app. Ask a staff member for help if needed.

  3. 03

    Monitor until the session ends.

    Check charging status and complete the session before disconnecting, following the equipment instructions.
