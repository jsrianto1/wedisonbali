Before you buy.

How much is a Wedison electric motorcycle in Bali?

Bali on-the-road prices start at IDR 21,900,000 for the Bees, before promotions. Standard registration, plates and first-year vehicle tax are included. Progressive tax and optional insurance are excluded. Compare model prices.

Where can I charge a Wedison in Bali?

Of 20 listed Bali locations, 4 were confirmed operating in Wedison SuperCharge app screenshots on 7 November 2026: Gedung By Pas 55, Gedung E-Sanur, Gedung Tohpati and Gedung Malboro. The current status of the other 16 locations needs confirmation. Athena, Victory and EdPower support SuperCharge; Bees uses home charging. Check the app for current charger availability. See addresses and map.

How does battery coverage differ with BAAS?

Standard battery coverage is up to 3 years or 100,000 km. With BAAS, the motorcycle purchase and battery subscription are separate; battery coverage follows the active subscription. Check your warranty booklet for the terms. Check warranty terms.

Where can I arrange a test ride or ask about service?

Visit the Wedison Bali Experience Center at Jl. Gatot Subroto Tengah No. 93, Dangin Puri Kaja, North Denpasar, Bali 80118. Contact the Bali team on +62 819 5693 282 to confirm appointments and motorcycle availability. Directions to the showroom.

What does an electric scooter cost to run each day?

The cost depends on distance, energy consumption and your electricity tariff. Enter your daily distance in the Wedison Bali calculator to compare estimated electricity and petrol costs. Estimate your riding costs.

Can I rent an electric scooter in Bali?

For electric scooter rentals in Bali, choose your scooter and travel dates with Werigo. Rates and availability depend on your location and rental period. Check rentals at Werigo.

Which models offer an Extended version?

Athena and Victory come in Regular and Extended versions. Compare battery capacity, range and Bali retail prices before choosing a variant. See model variants.
