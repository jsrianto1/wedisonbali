Before you buy.

What does the Bali OTR price include?

Includes STNK registration, BPKB ownership documents, standard plates and first-year vehicle tax. Excludes progressive tax where applicable and optional insurance. Confirm price and availability when ordering.

Does every model support SuperCharge?

Athena, Victory and EdPower support SuperCharge. Bees uses home charging.

Should I choose Regular or Extended?

Athena and Victory offer Regular and Extended variants. Check battery capacity, range and prices on each model page or ask during your test ride.
